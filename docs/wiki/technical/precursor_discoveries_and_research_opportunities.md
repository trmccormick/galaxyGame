# Precursor Discoveries & Research Opportunities
> **Wiki section:** Technical · **Status:** DIRECTION — future possibility only; no precursor research system, technology unlock, or technology-tree change is approved by this page.

## Purpose

Some unusual discoveries may provide material for human investigation. Depending on what is found and what the existing technology tree already contains, a discovery might:
- provide evidence or data that helps advance an existing research path;
- offer a new research opportunity if the existing tree has no suitable path;
- reveal a capability humans might not otherwise have pursued; or
- remain an interesting discovery with no practical technology outcome.

These are possible outcomes, not promised rewards. Discovery does not automatically grant technology, and there is no requirement that every mystery be explained.

## Review the existing technology tree first

The existing technology tree may be incomplete or may not be fully represented in current documentation. Before adding branches or assigning research rewards to discoveries, audit what actually exists.

The review should establish:
1. The current technology tree, including implemented technologies, documented intent, prerequisites, and dependencies.
2. Which technologies are implemented, planned, proposed, obsolete, or merely mentioned in old brainstorming.
3. Whether a candidate discovery could support an existing research path, accelerate it, or reveal a genuinely missing capability.
4. Whether a proposed new branch would duplicate or conflict with existing technology.

Only after that audit should individual discovery-to-research relationships be considered. No new branch, prerequisite, unlock, or research reward is established here.

## Candidate discovery opportunities

### Alpha Centauri jumpgate relic

The Alpha Centauri concept raises a possible transportation research direction distinct from the existing infrastructure-based AWS network. The source inspiration distinguishes fixed jumpgate infrastructure from ships carrying their own jump capability.

Potential research questions, not decisions:
- Could recovered evidence point to a different way of accessing or traversing space?
- Would any resulting human capability require fixed infrastructure, or could it be self-contained aboard a ship?
- Would this be compatible with, complementary to, or wholly separate from wormholes and AWS?

No hyperspace model, alternate dimension, gate network, ship drive, destination behavior, or operational rule is defined. The existing wormhole/AWS architecture remains the current design.

Alpha Centauri may also serve as a reason to expand AWS into the local stellar neighborhood, but the relic does not determine how AWS expansion or natural-wormhole discovery works.

### Wolf 359 battlefield remnants

The concept explicitly includes derelict ships, advanced weaponry and defensive systems, salvageable data cores, and possible tactical recordings. These materials could become research inputs after recovery and analysis.

Possible research value is not yet mapped to technology categories or specific unlocks. The first step is to inspect the existing technology tree and then determine whether any recovered information fits an existing path or justifies a new one. The site is an exploration/salvage opportunity; GalaxyGame does not have combat.

### Other ambiguous structures or signals

The Cydonia mystery, Reaper-like sighting, and Protomolecule-like structure may support investigation, data collection, or narrative uncertainty. They do not currently have approved technology rewards. They may remain unexplained.

Europa's subsurface ocean is a scientific milestone and could support further planetary/oceanographic or astrobiological investigation, but it does not imply life or precursor technology.

## Keep the wormhole technology model distinct

Potential precursor transportation discoveries must not blur the existing natural-wormhole and AWS concepts:

- **Natural wormholes are inherently unstable.** Sol's mouth is more stable because of its specific placement at Jupiter–Sun L3, where Jupiter provides a natural gravitational counterbalance. This is a location-specific advantage, not a general property of natural wormholes.
- **EM stabilization** is developed by Sol after the Eden-side natural wormhole snaps. It can sustain Sol's natural connection and other natural wormhole connections from Sol for as long as needed.
- **AWS artificial counterbalance** can improve the stability of a natural wormhole when deployed opposite it, especially where a suitable natural gravitational counterbalance is absent.
- These are complementary mechanisms: natural gravitational counterbalance, active EM stabilization, and AWS artificial counterbalance are not interchangeable.
- The B'Joran pair is a unique Easter egg exception and is not evidence that ordinary natural wormholes are stable or exempt from EM economics.

Do not infer unconfirmed network-generation, discovery, or expansion rules from any Easter egg.
