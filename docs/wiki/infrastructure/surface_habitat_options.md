# Surface Habitat Options

**Status**: DIRECTION / OPEN  
**Last Updated**: 2026-11-04  
**Related**: [settlement_progression_and_footholds.md](../player/settlement_progression_and_footholds.md), [power_options.md](power_options.md), atmospheric harvesting and settlement infrastructure pages

This page is the wiki home for surface (and near-surface) habitat design once power, logistics, and industrial base are mature. Early footholds remain lava-tube or heavily bermed; those are documented elsewhere and are treated here only as the prerequisite stage.

Useful prior material on sealed volumes, regolith construction, and early habitation has been harvested. The suburban-scale high-power package and the clear power-density gate are the main additive direction from the 2026-11-04 brainstorm.

---

## Progression Gate

**DIRECTION**

Surface suburban-scale habitats become available only after:

1. A secure subterranean or bermed foothold already exists.
2. Local ISRU (regolith processing, basalt fiber, basic binders) is operational.
3. Reliable high-density power is available (orbital beaming preferred — see power_options.md).
4. Critical stockpiles of volatiles and structural materials are healthy.

Until those conditions are met, surface activity is limited to extraction, surveying, and temporary pressurized modules. This keeps early game focused on survival and industrial bootstrap.

## High-Power Homestead (Preferred Near-Term Direction)

**DIRECTION** — enabled by grid-scale or orbital-beamed power.

### Overall Concept
A private aerogel-composite dome covering a single upper-class suburban-scale homestead (roughly 3 500–5 000 ft² living area plus yard/pool footprint). The dome provides the primary pressure envelope; the house itself is a secondary pressure vessel and radiation/thermal mass.

### Atmosphere
- Target: ~10.2 psi (ISS-style) with slightly enriched oxygen fraction to maintain Earth-normal partial pressure.
- Generation: Integrated external intake + separation plant.
  - Compresses raw local atmosphere (Mars or equivalent).
  - Strips CO₂ (routed to MOXIE-style solid-oxide electrolysis for O₂).
  - Captures native N₂ + Ar (and trace noble gases) as buffer gas.
  - Blends and injects into the dome volume.
- High-pressure storage tanks for O₂ and buffer gas provide redundancy during maintenance or dust events.

### Structure
- House: 3D-printed from local processed regolith (geopolymer or sintered basalt) with continuous basalt-fiber reinforcement.
- Utility chases printed in place.
- Windows and exterior doors: high-precision imported transparent composites seated in printed rebates with elastomeric seals.
- Interior doors and finishes: local composites or foamed basalt.
- Acoustic treatment required (porous regolith-fiber panels) to avoid cave-like echo.

### Dome
- Aerogel-core sandwich panel (local silica source preferred once manufacturing matures).
- Provides passive greenhouse heating, UV attenuation, and thermal isolation.
- Structural ribs: sintered or spun basalt anchored into bedrock.
- Exterior dust-cleaning mechanism required for long-term light transmission.

### Water & Thermal
- Indoor pool acts as thermal flywheel and humidity source.
- Closed-loop HVAC recovers evaporated water and returns it to the pool or domestic system.
- Subsurface ice or hydrated-mineral extraction supplies initial fill; thereafter the system is nearly closed.

### Biological Systems (Lawn / Garden)
- Ground under the dome is first leveled, compacted, and perchlorate-washed (or equivalent local toxin removal).
- Thin layer of inoculated topsoil (processed regolith fines + organic humus from digesters) supports grass and kitchen garden.
- Lawn and garden function as active life-support nodes:
  - CO₂ scrub and O₂ production.
  - Final biological filter for treated septic effluent (sub-surface drip irrigation).
  - Transpiration moisture recovered by HVAC.
- Excess CO₂ can be metered from the intake plant when plant demand exceeds human production; excess O₂ can be vented or stored.
- Human biowaste is processed through anaerobic digesters / composters; nutrients return to the soil loop. Methane byproduct can be stored for nighttime heat or recreational fire-pit use.

### Garage / Transition
- Attached or integrated garage bay serves as primary vehicle airlock and decontamination zone.
- Rover drives in, outer door seals, dust is cleared, occupants exit into the pressurized dome volume and walk into the house via a mudroom door.

## Constrained-Power Alternative

**OPEN**

When only low-kilowatt surface solar or small RTG-class power is available the design changes sharply:

- Habitat retreats underground or under thick regolith berm.
- Lower total pressure regime (~5.0–5.6 psi, higher O₂ fraction) to reduce compression work.
- Vertical photobioreactors or dense micro-green walls replace horizontal lawns.
- No open pool; water stored in heavily insulated tanks.
- Passive phase-change thermal storage preferred over active heating.
- Daylight delivered by light pipes or fiber optics rather than a large translucent dome.

This variant is more appropriate for early surface outposts or power-constrained sites.

## Open Questions / Future Work

- Exact passive temperature rise achievable with realistic aerogel thickness under local insolation.
- Mass and complexity of the integrated atmosphere plant versus distributed smaller units.
- Long-term dust mitigation and exterior maintenance cadence.
- Genetic or selective breeding targets for low-light turf and food crops.
- Safety systems for open-flame (methane fire-pit) inside a closed volume.
- Whether multiple homesteads can share a single large dome versus private domes.
- Minimum continuous power required to support one high-power homestead versus a multi-homestead complex.

All items remain **DIRECTION** or **OPEN** until physics models, manufacturing pathways, and acceptance as established intent.
