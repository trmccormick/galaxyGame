## ESTABLISHED INTENT (owner, 2026-11-04)

- **This page records a thought exercise.** The owner explored how the game's systems could work together inside a pressurized dome around a large house. A house like that is **not** a planned structure in the game. It was a way to test ideas.
- **The pressurization and oxygen setup from the exercise is considered a good approach.** See the next section.
- The exercise assumes **plenty of power** (grid power from a fission or fusion source, or beamed or orbital solar power). Without it the habitat would be different (see *Without plenty of power*).
- The first outposts and settlements are basic, and the first settlements would be in lava tubes. A sealed and pressurized lava tube gives settlers protection from explosive decompression. Labs and housing units have airlocks.

---

## The atmosphere setup the owner liked (DIRECTION)

Presented as one combined system:

1. A pump pulls in outside air (Mars or similar) and separates out the CO₂.
2. The CO₂ goes to a MOXIE-type unit, which makes the oxygen.
3. The remaining native gases (nitrogen, argon and others) are pumped into the dome as buffer gas.
4. The dome is filled to about **80% of the pressure it needs**. That is **not** Earth pressure. The reference is an ISS-style pressure.
5. If oxygen gets too high, the system vents it. If CO₂ gets too low, the system injects it.
6. Tanks store oxygen and buffer gas. Methane produced by the system is also pulled out and stored.

## Other points from the exercise (DIRECTION)

- Build from **local processed regolith** and import only electronics and other hard-to-make items. The house in the exercise is mostly 3D-printed regolith, finished with imported items such as windows and doors.
- The ground under the dome is processed first.
- A lawn or garden is not only decoration. It feeds the life-support and waste-recycling systems, and human waste can serve as fertilizer. A septic-style system could work as on Earth.
- A garage needs access to both the outside and the inside.

## Without plenty of power (DIRECTION)

The owner said a large house wouldn't be possible without a large power grid. Without it, the habitat goes under a berm or underground, and its design differs. See [Power Options](power_options.md) for the power sources under discussion.
