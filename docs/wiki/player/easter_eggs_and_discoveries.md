# Optional Discoveries & Easter Eggs
> **Wiki section:** Player · **Status:** DESIGN INTENT — optional narrative and exploration concepts; individual entries are not implementation commitments.

## Purpose and scope

GalaxyGame may include optional discoveries, references, mysteries, scientific milestones, historical records, and humorous anomalies. They are a flexible source of atmosphere and memorable exploration, not a required storyline or a checklist that must all be implemented.

These concepts were gathered from earlier flavor documentation and a later owner discussion. The older material contains brainstorms and implementation suggestions; this page records the useful concepts after review rather than treating every old proposal as approved design.

Guiding principles:
- Easter eggs are optional. They may be used, expanded, revised, or left unused.
- A discovery does not need to explain its origin or reward the player with technology.
- Mystery and ambiguity are valid outcomes. Do not force every event into a single connected narrative.
- The game does not establish that an alien civilization exists or is present. An unusual object, signal, structure, or encounter is not proof of alien life or its creators.
- Scientific discoveries, such as evidence for Europa's subsurface ocean, must remain distinct from evidence of life.
- GalaxyGame has no combat. Battlefield remnants are exploration and salvage content, not a combat mission.
- Existing wormhole and AWS architecture remains the current transportation framework. Easter eggs must not silently redefine it.

## Discovery concepts

### B'Joran wormhole pair — Star Trek: Deep Space Nine

**Owner-confirmed exception.** Retain this as a unique, campaign-level Easter egg. The pair simply exists; its origin is unexplained and should remain unexplained. Do not describe it as naturally occurring, constructed, or created by an ancient civilization.

Its defining exception properties are:
- Both ends are completely and permanently stable; instability is zero.
- It emits no EM and has no EM requirement.
- It has no stabilization cost and requires no EM stabilization, Jupiter-like gravitational counterbalance, or AWS counterbalance.
- It is excluded from normal EM economics.
- It is extremely rare and globally/campaign unique: one occurrence.

This is a deliberate special case, not a precedent for ordinary natural wormholes. Do not generalize its stability or economics to the normal wormhole network.

### Alpha Centauri jumpgate relic — Babylon 5

An exploration discovery at Alpha Centauri involving remnants described in the source concept as jumpgate rings, control crystals, and related data. The idea raises the possibility that a different transportation technology could exist alongside the current wormhole/AWS network.

The reference suggests two broad concepts worth retaining for later exploration: fixed gate infrastructure, and ships with self-contained jump capability. It does not decide whether either is possible in GalaxyGame, how gates would connect, whether destinations are fixed, what a ship drive could do, or what physics, costs, limits, or research would be involved. Those are open questions, not mechanics.

Alpha Centauri is also an interesting potential discovery destination because reaching it may require AWS expansion into the local stellar neighborhood. That possibility does not establish a general AWS expansion or natural-wormhole discovery rule.

### Wolf 359 battlefield remnants — Star Trek: The Next Generation

A possible exploration and salvage site containing derelict vessels, advanced weaponry and defensive systems, data cores, and potentially tactical recordings. It may offer research opportunities, but no specific technology unlocks have been selected.

The concept could give players a reason to extend AWS reach into the local stellar neighborhood. It remains a salvage/discovery opportunity, not a combat scenario. Any later research value depends on reviewing the existing technology tree first.

### Cydonia formation mystery — real-world Face on Mars / Mission to Mars inspiration

The baseline interpretation is a natural geological formation shaped by erosion. The optional mystery is whether detailed investigation might reveal subtle evidence of deliberate modification. Neither artificial alteration nor a precursor presence is established.

Keep the ambiguity. Geological analysis, imaging, and investigation can provide the experience without requiring a definitive answer.

### Reaper-like long-range sighting — Mass Effect inspiration

A rare, unexplained sensor encounter with a massive, unusual vessel at extreme range, followed by an anomalous drive signature and transient effects reported by the crew. This is a brief sighting, not first contact.

Do not confirm the identity, origin, species, threat, or existence of a Reaper civilization. It may remain an unexplained log entry with no technology reward.

### Protomolecule-like structure — The Expanse inspiration

A strange, luminous, highly ordered structure associated with an asteroid and apparently capable of reorganizing material. It is an investigation concept, not confirmation of its creators, purpose, or an alien civilization.

Any computational, energetic, or material capabilities described in the original brainstorm remain unverified narrative possibilities, not established game mechanics.

### Infinite Improbability event — Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy inspiration

A deliberately absurd, brief reality-bending encounter: improbable ship/crew reports, a passing unusual vessel, a Don't Panic message, or a depressed-robot-style reaction. Its purpose is humor and surprise, not a new simulation subsystem.

### Europa subsurface ocean discovery

A scientific exploration milestone in which a probe reports measurements consistent with a subsurface liquid-water ocean beneath Europa's ice and prepares astrobiological sampling. This is a discovery of an environment of scientific interest, not a discovery of extraterrestrial life.

### Babylon 5 diplomatic archive

A recovered historical or archival record inspired by Babylon 5: a station log about diplomacy, neutral territory, and the hope of preventing conflict, with an unsettling warning in the background. It is an atmospheric historical artifact, not a commitment to a new diplomatic system or a confirmed external threat.

## Use and implementation boundaries

- These entries are a pool of optional concepts, not a mandatory feature list.
- Do not copy old rarity values, triggers, integration points, or implementation details into runtime behavior without separate review.
- Do not introduce direct franchise names, protected character identities, quotations, or trademarks into player-facing content by default. Treat source titles here as internal inspiration labels; review actual in-game wording separately.
- Do not let an Easter egg change the established wormhole/AWS architecture by implication.
- If an entry is developed into a gameplay feature, record the specific owner-approved rules here before implementation and identify unresolved details.

## Relationship to precursor research

Some discoveries may eventually provide evidence, materials, data, or concepts that humans can investigate and perhaps use to extend existing capabilities. That possibility is deliberately separate from the discovery catalogue. See [Precursor Discoveries & Research Opportunities](../technical/precursor_discoveries_and_research_opportunities.md).
