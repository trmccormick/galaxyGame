## PROMPT

```
Generate a single RH-400 Regolith Harvester Rover for GalaxyGame, a hard-sci-fi industrial space settlement simulation game.

SUBJECT:
A Mk2 autonomous regolith harvesting rover — a heavy-duty mobile industrial vehicle designed to skim planetary surfaces and gather regolith for in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) processing. The vehicle has a low-profile rectangular chassis with a forward-mounted regolith skimming scoop, a mid-body cylindrical processing canister, a rear-mounted dust exhaust stack, a top-mounted sensor mast with rotating array, and six visible drive wheels on an independent suspension chassis. Exposed hydraulic actuator arms connect the scoop to the chassis at the joints.

PROPORTIONS (must be accurate):
- The vehicle is significantly longer than it is wide or tall — approximately 2.6 times longer than wide
- Low-profile silhouette: height is roughly equal to width, both much less than length
- Overall shape: elongated rectangular platform with forward scoop protrusion and rear exhaust stack

MANUFACTURING APPEARANCE (Mk2 — Precision Industrial Factory):
- Primary paneling: clean white or light-gray over the main body panels
- Mechanical undercarriage: dark gray to black chassis, suspension components, and wheel housings
- Surface finish: precision-machined surfaces with minimal visible construction seams; slight sheen consistent with factory-assembled aerospace equipment
- Tech level Mk2 characteristics: cleaner than early-generation equipment, fewer visible fasteners, welded joints, no visible layer lines, no 3D-printed rough texture, no improvised or bootstrap appearance
- Overall impression: heavy-duty, purpose-built, factory-assembled industrial vehicle — NASA/ESA-inspired aerospace-industrial aesthetic

MATERIALS (visually present):
- High-strength steel structural members (undercarriage, chassis frame)
- Aluminum structural panels (primary body paneling)
- Sealed hydraulic systems (visible actuator arms on scoop joints)
- Reinforced rubber tracks/wheels (six drive wheels with visible treads)

MARKINGS:
- Black and yellow hazard striping applied functionally to moving parts and edges only (scoop edges, exhaust stack rim)
- Unit identification stenciled on the hull (simple alphanumeric marking)

RECOGNITION FEATURES (all must be clearly visible):
1. Six-wheel independent suspension chassis — six drive wheels visible along the vehicle's sides
2. Forward regolith skimming scoop assembly — prominent protrusion at the front of the chassis
3. Mid-body cylindrical processing canister — rounded tank-like structure centered on the chassis
4. Rear-mounted dust exhaust stack — vertical stack at the rear of the vehicle
5. Top-mounted sensor mast with rotating array — tall thin mast rising from the top center, topped with a disc or ring-shaped sensor array
6. Exposed hydraulic actuator arms — visible cylindrical pistons connecting the scoop to the chassis

RENDER REQUIREMENTS:
- Single vehicle only, fully visible, no cropping
- Top-down orthographic camera view (directly above, looking straight down, no perspective distortion)
- Vehicle centered within the canvas, occupying approximately 75% of the frame
- Soft neutral studio lighting — cool, even, diffused illumination that highlights surface details without harsh shadows or specular highlights
- Physically accurate materials — steel, aluminum, rubber, and composite surfaces should look correct for each material
- Strong, immediately recognizable silhouette — the six-wheel chassis with forward scoop and rear stack must be clear even at small scale

BACKGROUND:
- Transparent background (alpha channel)
- No ground plane, no terrain, no shadows beyond a subtle contact shadow beneath the object
- No sky, no stars, no environment, no props, no dust effects
- No text, no labels, no annotations, no logos, no borders, no watermark

OUTPUT:
- Transparent PNG
- 1024×1024 pixels
- High resolution suitable for production game assets
```
